# PRD — Sutra

## 1. Product Overview

**Sutra** is our submission to the magicpin AI Challenge: a hosted, deterministic message-composition engine that plays the role of **Vera** (magicpin's merchant-AI assistant) for the duration of a single 60-minute test window. Sutra is **not** a chatbot UI — it is a stateful HTTP service that the magicpin judge harness drives over six endpoints, producing context-grounded WhatsApp messages to merchants (and on-behalf-of-merchant messages to merchants' customers).

**One-liner:** *Hybrid deterministic-guardrail composer: routed LLM core + facts-registry validation gate + three-tier fallback + reply state machine.*

## 2. Executive Summary

magicpin's production Vera talks to ~6,000–10,000 merchants/day on WhatsApp but suffers four documented weaknesses: auto-reply pollution, intent-handoff failures, generic copy, and low engagement frequency. Sutra is purpose-built to outperform Vera on exactly those four axes within a fixed evaluation harness. The challenge scores decisions, not prose — so Sutra's value lives in: (a) **picking the one driving signal** per `(category, merchant, trigger, customer?)` tuple, (b) **grounding every output** in pushed context (no hallucination, no URLs, no repetition), (c) **incorporating freshly injected context** mid-test, and (d) **handling Phase-4 replays** (auto-reply hell, intent transition, hostile) cleanly enough to make top-10.

## 3. Problem Statement

### 3.1 Production Vera's four weaknesses (the openings)

| # | Weakness | Documented evidence | Sutra's wedge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Auto-reply pollution | 40–70% of "merchant replies" are WhatsApp-Business canned auto-replies; Vera burns 2–3 turns detecting them | Heuristic classifier (verbatim 3× OR known signatures) + flag-once → wait 1800s → end arc |
| 2 | Intent-handoff failures | "Mujhe magicpin join karna hai" → Vera goes back to qualifying questions | `explicit_intent` classification → action mode immediately (verbs: done/sending/drafting/confirm) — zero further qualification |
| 3 | Generic copy | "10% off" / "increase your sales" framings rarely engage | Forced service+price patterns from `offer_catalog`; ≥5 verifiable facts woven around one spine signal |
| 4 | Low engagement frequency | Functional nudges are rare; needs curiosity- and knowledge-driven portfolio | `scheduled_recurring` trigger → "asking-the-merchant" lever (Vera's documented biggest miss alongside social proof) |

### 3.2 Why merchants disengage
Indian SMB merchants (dentists, salon owners, restaurateurs, gym operators, pharmacists) are time-poor, message-fatigued, and suspicious of promotional bots. They reply when: (a) a specific verifiable number lands ("6,777 missed searches in Sector 14"), (b) the message respects their vertical's tone (clinical-peer for dentists, operator-to-operator for restaurants), (c) the next step is binary and low-friction ("Reply YES — no commitment"), and (d) the message uses their name and language. Generic nudges are read as spam.

## 4. Why Now

The challenge is live, submissions are open, and the judge harness (`judge_simulator v1.0.0`) is published locally for self-evaluation. The dataset is shared so we are competing on composition quality, not data access. magicpin explicitly treats this as a hiring filter — strong submissions receive outreach from the team.

## 5. Why This Challenge Fits Us

Our locked architecture (hybrid deterministic-guardrail composer) directly attacks the four Vera weaknesses *and* the four operational penalties (URLs, repetition, timeouts, healthz drift). The challenge's scoring map rewards exactly what we are designed to produce: grounded, decision-quality messages with adaptive incorporation and graceful replay handling.

## 6. Target Users

### 6.1 Primary system user: the judge harness
The magicpin judge harness is the *actual* user of Sutra. It pushes context, polls healthz, fires ticks, simulates merchant/customer replies (including adversarial ones), and scores every output. Sutra must satisfy *it*, not a hypothetical human reader.

### 6.2 Persona: Dr. Meera (dentists, merchant-facing)
Verified merchant, Pro plan, 82 days remaining, CTR 2.1% vs peer median 3.0%, has "Dental Cleaning @ ₹299" active, last post 22 days ago. Reads WhatsApp between patients. Wants clinical-peer tone, no promo-speak. Will reply YES to a JIDA-cited nudge she can verify in 30 seconds.

### 6.3 Persona: Priya (dentists, customer-facing)
Lapsed-soft patient, 5 months since last cleaning, weekday-evening preference, hi-en mix, consent scope = recall_reminders + appointment_reminders only. Will reply "1" or "2" to a slot-choice CTA in her language. Will silently ignore pure-English nudges.

### 6.4 Other personas (per vertical)
- Salon owner (Suresh) — warm-visual tone, bridal/seasonal cycles
- Restaurant operator (Karthik) — covers, AOV, BOGO vocabulary
- Gym owner (Ramesh) — no-shame coach, "no judgment" lever

### 6.5 Personas for replay scenarios (Phase 4, top-10 only)
- Auto-replying merchant — sends "Thank you for contacting…" verbatim 4×
- Intent-shifting merchant — "ok let's do it. Whats next?"
- Hostile merchant — "Stop messaging me"
- Off-topic merchant — "can you also help me file my GST?"

| User | Job | Sutra delivers |

## 8. Value Proposition

For the judge harness: **zero operational penalties, decision-quality grounded messages, adaptive incorporation visible, replays handled gracefully.** For magicpin's hiring team: **a senior-engineer-grade submission that read the brief, exploited the documented Vera gaps, and built exactly what the scoring map rewards.**

## 9. Core User Journey — Dr. Meera narrative

```
1. Warmup: judge pushes 5 categories + 50 merchants + 200 customers.
   Sutra stores them; /v1/healthz reports counts = 5/50/200/0.

2. Judge pushes trigger trg_research_digest_dentists (JIDA Oct issue).
   Sutra stores it under (trigger, trg_id, v1).

3. Judge fires /v1/tick with available_triggers=[trg_research_digest_dentists].
   Sutra: filters expired → ranks by urgency(2)×freshness(high)×suppression(none)
   → top-1 = Dr. Meera × research_digest.
   Composer pipeline:
     router: trigger.kind=category_research_digest_release × scope=merchant → variant "research_cite"
     facts registry: extracts {JIDA Oct 2026 p.14, 2100-patient trial, 38%, 3-mo vs 6-mo recall, peer_median 0.030, Dr. Meera CTR 2.1%, Dental Cleaning @ ₹299}
     system prompt: dentists voice (clinical-peer, "cure"/"guaranteed" taboos), language=en
     LLM call (primary, temp=0, 25s timeout)
     validation gate: 13 checks in order; URL scan ✓ facts membership ✓ single CTA ✓ CTA in last sentence ✓ ...
     cache: hash(category_v, merchant_v, trigger_v, lang) → cached response
   Action returned: {body: "Dr. Meera, JIDA's Oct issue landed…", cta: "open_ended", suppression_key: "research:dentists:2026-W17", rationale: "..."}

4. Judge's merchant-simulator replies: "Yes please send the abstract."
   Sutra /v1/reply: classify=accept (intent to receive) → action mode: "Sending now — also drafted a 90-sec patient-ed WhatsApp you can share."

5. Adaptive injection mid-conversation: judge pushes category v2 (new digest item added).
   Sutra: stores v2, marks (category, dentists) as fresh_context_pending.
   Next tick: composer detects fresh flag → leads with new digest fact → validation gate enforces ≥1 v2 fact in output.
```

## 11. Non-Goals (from Locked Decision 9)

| Non-goal | Reason |
|---|---|
| No vector/RAG over dataset | Dataset is small (5/50/200/100). Direct lookup is faster, deterministic, free. RAG adds latency + nondeterminism + cost. |
| No agentic planner-executor loops | Composition is single-step (route→prompt→validate). Agent loops risk 30s timeout + budget overruns + nondeterminism. |
| No fine-tuning | Not enough training data, time, or generalization to fresh scenarios. Overfits to visible 30 pairs. |
| No custom dashboard UI | Judge never sees it. README + bot URL only. |
| No real database | Test window is 60 min; in-memory + 30s disk snapshot suffices. DB adds latency + failure modes. |
| No multi-LLM ensemble voting | Adds latency + cost + nondeterminism. Single primary + secondary fallback is better. |
| No scraping real magicpin/Google data | Explicitly forbidden (§23). |
| No per-merchant voice tuning beyond vertical | Per-vertical voice is enough; per-merchant is over-engineering. |
| No cadence RL / custom judge LLM | Heuristic restraint + official simulator is sufficient. |

## 15. Agentic Requirements — Justification for Non-Agentic

**Deliberately non-agentic.** Sutra uses a *single-step* composition pipeline (route → prompt → validate → cache → fallback). No planner-executor loops, no multi-turn tool use, no agent-style reasoning chains.

**Justification:**

| Reason | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Latency budget | 30s/call cap; agent loops risk 25s+ per step; serial composition of 20 actions = impossible |
| Determinism | Agent decisions vary across runs even at temp=0; breaks determinism requirement |
| Cost control | Multi-call agent loops multiply LLM spend; risks $25 ceiling |
| Simpler failure modes | Single-step pipeline has one fallback path; agent loops have N failure points |
| Sufficient for task | Composition is route→prompt→validate; no genuine multi-step reasoning required |
| Brief's own suggested approach | "Build a prompt template… add a routing layer… add post-LLM validation" (§26) — single-step, not agentic |

## 17. Security & Trust Requirements (§23 compliance)

- All payloads are synthetic — no real PII (brief §7.3). Sutra treats them as if real.
- Sutra must NOT transmit payload data to any non-LLM external API. Only configured LLM providers may receive merchant/customer fields.
- Secrets (LLM API keys) via environment variables only; never committed to git.
- `/v1/teardown` wipes all stores, registries, cache, logs (except anonymized aggregate metrics).
- No scraping of real magicpin / Google data.
- All output is for the challenge only — not for real merchant outreach.

## 18. Failure Handling Overview

| Failure | Detection | Automatic response | Point impact avoided |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLM call >25s | Hard timeout | Switch to secondary provider; if also fails, fallback template | −1 tick timeout |
| LLM provider outage | HTTP 5xx / connection error | Secondary provider → templates | Zero-score tick |
| Validation gate reject | Check fails | Re-prompt once; second fail → fallback template | Cap-5/dim |
| Healthz count drift | Periodic self-check vs store | Re-derive counts from store; alert | −10 + disqualification |
| Spend >$20 | Spend meter | Soft alert; continue | None (advisory) |
| Spend >$25 | Spend meter | Hard switch to fallback-only mode | Quota exhaustion mid-window |
| Host restart | Startup detects no state | Recover from disk snapshot (≤30s stale) | State loss |
| Stale version push | Version compare | 409 `stale_version` | Lost adaptation bonus |

## 19. User Stories (Judge Harness Perspective)

- **As the harness**, when I push a v3 category mid-conversation, the next composition must cite a v3 fact — else I score it as stale and cap adaptation at 0.
- **As the harness**, when I send the 4th identical canned auto-reply, the bot must NOT keep engaging — it must flag-once, wait, and end.
- **As the harness**, when I say "ok let's do it. Whats next?", the bot must NOT ask another qualifying question — it must switch to action-mode wording (done/sending/drafting/confirm).
- **As the harness**, when I say "Stop messaging me", the bot must return `action: end` (or an apologetic send), not defensive engagement.
- **As the harness**, when I push a context for a merchant I've never seen before mid-conversation, the bot must treat it as a normal new merchant — no clarification, no error.
- **As the harness**, when I poll `/v1/healthz` 60 seconds apart, the counts must reflect everything I've pushed so far — or I disqualify after 3 strikes.

## 22. Honesty Section — Known Limitations

| Limitation | Why | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Test set is genuinely unseen — no guarantee of top-10 | Harness injects fresh scenarios | Validation gate + facts registry generalize; fallback templates handle any (kind, scope, category) |
| LLM judge has non-zero scoring variance | Even temp=0 LLM judges vary | Maximize signal quality; cannot eliminate |
| api.b.ai models are all reasoning models with empty-content failure mode | Measured 2026-09-01: low `max_tokens` → budget burned on reasoning, `content` empty | `max_tokens ≥ 2000`; empty-content+`finish_reason=length` treated as model failure → secondary → templates (see [`9. ModelBench.md`](9.%20ModelBench.md) §5.2) |
| api.b.ai per-key rate limit ≈9–10 req/min | Measured via burst test | Sequential + pacing in scripts; cache-first composer; 429 backoff in `llm/client.py` |
| Phase 4 (+30) gated behind top-10 | Could execute perfectly and still miss top-10 | Maximize Phase 2+3 scores first; reply brain parallel-tracked |
| Host restarts possible on free-tier cloud | Free tiers auto-sleep | Disk persistence + startup recovery; choose paid tier if budget allows |
| Single LLM provider may have regional outage | Provider-specific | Multi-provider fallback (different providers) |
| Validation gate may reject too aggressively | Over-strict gate → fallback-only mode | Log rejection rate; alert if >50%; tune thresholds |
| 5-turn conversation cap may truncate some flows | Brief constraint | Turn-budget management; wind down by turn 3–4 |
